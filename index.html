Rooted Task Management App

Motivation

Neurodivergent users, particularly autistic users and folks with ADHD, indicated a need for support specific to their neurotype. The emergence of anxiety as a theme indicates that users need an app that actively combats anxiety both aesthetically and by providing organization tools that can help quiet a busy mind.

  • Topic Modeling
  • Prototypes
  • Survey
Support area Goal Interfaces and activities
Executive function support Support executive functioning.
  1. Manually create to-do lists
  2. Have AI break down textual brain dumps into actionable tasks
  3. Schedule tasks and send the user reminders
Rewards and Motivation Create a gamified, motivating experience.
  1. Appealing visuals
  2. Virtual pet mechanic
  3. Rewards for self or virtual pet, e.g., clothes
Mental Health Support Provide mental health support that facilitates productivity.
  1. White noise
  2. Journaling
  3. Meditation
  4. Gratitude

Home Blood Pressure Cuff Redesign

Overview

A redesign of the home blood pressure cuff interface to improve usability and user experience for patients monitoring their health.

Design Goals

  • Simplify user interface
  • Improve accessibility
  • Enhance data visualization
  • Reduce measurement errors

Approach

User-centered design with extensive testing and feedback from real users.
